Is ManagR really free?
acceptedAnswer
Yes. ManagR is 100% free to use — no subscription fees, no hidden charges. We believe in empowering property owners with the best tools without any cost barrier. You can use all features of ManagR for free, forever.
How does automated rent collection work in ManagR?
ManagR automatically sends WhatsApp reminders to tenants with a payment link before and on the due date. When tenants pay via UPI or card, the payment status updates instantly in your dashboard. You no longer need to make manual rent calls or do bank reconciliation.
What types of properties can I manage with ManagR?
ManagR supports PGs (paying guest accommodations), hostels, co-living spaces, flats, and multi-property rental businesses. The platform manages at the bed level — you can track each bed's occupancy, revenue, and status individually.
How do I onboard tenants and collect KYC documents?
ManagR provides paperless tenant onboarding — you can verify Aadhaar identity in real time, generate digitally signed rental agreements, and store all KYC documents securely in the cloud. The process takes under 5 minutes.
How does ManagR help me fill vacant beds faster?
ManagR consolidates leads from BedR, 99acres, MagicBricks, NoBroker, and walk-ins into one unified CRM. It detects duplicate enquiries, tags sources, and lets you schedule property visits with automated WhatsApp confirmations — reducing no-shows and converting enquiries faster.
How quickly can I set up ManagR for my property?
Setting up ManagR takes under 5 minutes. You add your buildings, import your tenants, and the platform is ready. No complex manuals or lengthy onboarding required.
Can I manage multiple properties with ManagR?
Yes. ManagR is built for multi-property operators. You can manage all your buildings, floors, rooms, and beds from a single dashboard with a real-time colour-coded occupancy view.
